Key Differences

B — Orbital Radar

  • Strongest cinematic identity — the sweep line and orbital dots feel like a real command bridge
  • Radar naturally maps to "scanning/monitoring" metaphor
  • Animated sweep line draws the eye
  • Stage positions on the ring are less intuitive than linear order — needs labels
  • Works best as a compact widget (sidebar or hero); gets sparse at full width
  • Mobile: radar shrinks well but info panel needs stacking

C — Segmented Progress

  • Instantly readable — left to right, filled to empty, done
  • Segmented bar scales perfectly from mobile to ultrawide
  • Stage log gives Grafana-level detail without leaving the card
  • More conventional — cinematic feel comes from the glow/animation treatment, not the shape
  • Expandable: collapsed = just the bar + status, expanded = full stage log
  • Mobile: bar stacks naturally, metadata cards go single column
